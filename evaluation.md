## Evaluation

The grading scheme below will be used to grade the design project
reports. Understand your designs thoroughly and write the best report
you can.

| Evaluation Item | Maximum Points |
| --- | --- |
| **Week 2 and 4 Check-ins** | **10** |
| **Report, Techniques & Results** |  |
| Design Equations/Analysis for each block | 15 |
| Transistor Level Design and Design Techniques | 15 |
| Design Issues and Tradeoffs | 10 |
| Simulation Results and Techniques | 15 |
| Achievement of Specs | 15 |
| Minimization of power consumption | 10 |
| Overall Effort/Creativity | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |
